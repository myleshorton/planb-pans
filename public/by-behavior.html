Field Guide · Start with what you see

Start with a behavior.

Most parents don’t arrive knowing “it’s Bartonella.” They arrive with rage, or OCD, or a kid who suddenly can’t eat. So start there. Pick what you’re seeing, and this points you to the drivers worth investigating — and the guide for each.

I walked this part of the labyrinth myself — knocked on the doors, read the research, and came back with the map. You don’t have to find the way out alone.

Read this first: a behavior doesn’t tell you the cause — it tells you where to look. The same rage can come from strep, a tick-borne infection, low blood sugar, or mast cells. This is a map of what to investigate, not a diagnosis. The only way to know is to test. Minta can take your child’s actual picture and tell you which of these to chase first.

Rage & aggression ›

Where to look: strep / PANDAS · Bartonella (the classic “Bartonella rage,” often with anxiety + nightmares) · blood-sugar swings (hangry meltdowns) · mast cell / histamine · pyroluria & copper/zinc (methylation). The Protocol · Lyme & Bartonella · MCAS · Methylation

OCD & intrusive thoughts ›

Where to look: strep / PANDAS (sudden, overnight OCD is the signature) · gut / Clostridia (p-cresol → dopamine dysregulation — why an SSRI alone keeps failing) · Lyme / Bartonella · cerebral folate deficiency (the FRAT test). The Protocol · Gut · Lyme · Folate / FRAT

Tics ›

Where to look: strep / PANDAS · Mycoplasma · PANS generally · low magnesium / mineral imbalance · Lyme. The Protocol · Lyme · Minerals

Anxiety, panic & separation fear ›

Where to look: strep · Bartonella · mold / mycotoxins · mast cell / histamine · thiamine deficiency / dysautonomia · methylation (overmethylation) · gut. Mold · MCAS · Methylation / thiamine · Bartonella · Gut

Sudden regression or speech loss ›

Where to look: cerebral folate deficiency (run the FRAT — a missed, treatable cause of regression) · a PANS flare · mold · an active infection. Folate / FRAT · The Protocol · Mold

Sensory overwhelm & reactivity ›

Where to look: mast cell / histamine · methylation · gut · mold · a dysregulated nervous system. MCAS · Methylation · Gut · Nervous-system regulation

Sleep trouble & night waking ›

Where to look: active infection / inflammation · low iron / ferritin (restless sleep) · mast cell (the histamine that peaks at night) · Bartonella (nightmares) · methylation. The Protocol · MCAS · Bartonella · Iron / methylation

Food refusal & restriction ›

Where to look: this is a core PANS symptom (fear of choking, contamination, or a sudden drop in intake — not pickiness) · gut discomfort · sensory. The Protocol · Gut · Nervous-system

Handwriting / fine-motor decline ›

Where to look: a sudden drop in handwriting is one of the most specific signs of PANS — take it seriously as an immune flare marker. The Protocol · The Full Map

Air hunger / drenching sweats ›

Where to look: air hunger (“can’t get a full breath”) + night sweats is the classic Babesia signature — the most-missed co-infection. Lyme & co-infections (Babesia)

Urinary frequency or bed-wetting ›

Where to look: new urinary frequency/enuresis is a recognized PANS symptom · also seen with Bartonella / infection. The Protocol · Bartonella

Brain fog & fatigue ›

Where to look: mold / CIRS · thiamine deficiency · methylation / detox backlog · active infection. Mold · Methylation / thiamine
